How HealLYF Works

1 Create Your Health Profile

Sign up and complete your health profile with medical history, allergies, and current medications to help doctors provide better care.

2 Describe Your Symptoms

Enter your symptoms, and our AI system will help match you with the right specialist or general physician for your condition.

3 Connect with a Doctor

Schedule an appointment or get immediate assistance from available doctors. Our translation services ensure smooth communication.

4 Receive Treatment Plan

Get prescriptions, diet recommendations, and follow-up schedules, all managed securely within the HealLYF platform.

5 Order Medicines & Book Tests

Use your unique prescription code to order medicines or book diagnostic tests with our partner network.

Bringing Healthcare to Rural & Semi-Urban Areas

Rural areas face significant healthcare challenges due to limited access, transportation issues, and financial constraints. HealLYF addresses these problems with dedicated solutions.

  • Automated voice-based services for non-smartphone users
  • Eliminating the need for internet access in rural areas
  • Drone-based medicine delivery to remote locations
  • Mobile diagnostic services and telemedicine units
  • Flexible payment options and subsidy integration
